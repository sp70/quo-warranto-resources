# Constitution Reference Documents

## In This Folder

### American_Constitution.pdf
About America: The Constitution of the United States with Explanatory Notes
(World Book Encyclopedia, 2004 edition · 93 pages)
Full text + annotated version of Constitution + all 27 amendments.
USE: Primary text source for organic vs. de facto comparison rows.

### Secret Proceedings and Debates_LoC.pdf
*Secret Proceedings and Debates of the Federal Convention* (1839, Library of Congress copy)
From notes by Robert Yates (Chief Justice, NY) and John Lansing (Chancellor, NY)
Also contains Luther Martin's "Genuine Information" to Maryland legislature
Published Richmond, VA · Wilbur Curtiss · 356 pages
USE: The framers' ACTUAL debates — what they said in private vs. what was published.
Key for the "what was intended vs. what we got" framing on the comparison page.
Also contains explicit discussions of commercial/banking concerns driving the Convention.
Direct LoC URL: https://lccn.loc.gov/11009690

### The Constitution and the Public Trust - Robert Natelson.pdf
Robert G. Natelson · Buffalo Law Review Vol. 52 (2004) · 102 pages
"[G]overnments are in fact...agents and trustees of the people" — James Madison
Covers: fiduciary duties of government officials, constitutional approval process,
founders' political/legal canon (Plato, Aristotle, Cicero, English common law)
USE: Academic anchor for the trust/fiduciary argument — connects to the QW site's
commercial entity findings. Natelson's trustee framing = exact bridge between
the organic Constitution and what the oath cases are actually about.
